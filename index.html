关于 About

纪录片作曲人出身的音乐制作人，用二十余年音乐历程，为影像创作专业配乐 Documentary composer turned music producer, crafting scores for visual storytelling

纪录片作曲人 Documentary Composer

罗领出生于湖南长沙——中国话剧奠基人田汉的故乡。从小学习声乐与民乐，自学音乐制作与作曲。 Born in Changsha, Hunan — hometown of Tian Han, pioneer of modern Chinese drama. Self-taught in music production and composition.

2023年，为芒果TV纪念抗美援朝战争胜利70周年纪录片《超时空寻找》创作主题曲《当你年轻的时候》。该纪录片由国家广播电视总局网络视听司特别指导。 In 2023, composed the theme song for Mango TV documentary "Beyond Time," commemorating the 70th anniversary of the Korean War victory.

2022年，受邀参加CCTV-3《为爱而歌》节目。同年担任优酷《超感新电音》专业评审。 In 2022, performed on CCTV-3 and served as professional judge for Youku's electronic music program.
