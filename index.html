About

关于 About

出生于湖南长沙，自学音乐制作与作曲二十余年。2023年为芒果TV纪录片《超时空寻找》创作主题曲，该片由国家广播电视总局特别指导。2022年受邀CCTV-3《为爱而歌》。 Born in Changsha, self-taught composer with over two decades of experience. Composed theme for Mango TV documentary "Beyond Time" in 2023, guided by SARFT. Performed on CCTV-3 in 2022.
